The Hidden Symphony of Rust
Rust isn't decay—it's chemistry's slow negotiation. When iron meets oxygen, electrons leap. The iron oxidizes; the oxygen reduces. Water acts as electrolyte, a molecular matchmaker. What looks like destruction is actually creation: iron(III) oxide, Fe₂O₃, a mineral with its own crystalline beauty. Mars is red because of rust. Your blood is red because of iron. Every bridge, every ship, every old nail tells this story. Rust prevention? Simply interruption—paint blocks oxygen, sacrificial zinc offers itself instead. Chemistry as diplomacy, negotiated one electron at a time.
